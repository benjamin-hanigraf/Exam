# CA Intermediate study material (ICAI)

Source: https://boslive.icai.org → Study Material (New Scheme) → Intermediate → English,
edition "Applicable for May 2026 Examination Onwards". Downloaded 30 Oct 2026.

Layout: `group-N/<paper>/module-M/<file>.pdf`. `00-initial-pages.pdf` is the front matter of each module;
`chNN-...` are chapters; `-uN-` marks units where ICAI splits a chapter into separate files.
`manifest.json` has the source URL, size and cover-page edition text for every file.

## Edition check
- ICAI's listing labels Papers 1, 2, 4, 5, 6A and 6B "Applicable for May 2026 Examination Onwards"
  (6A's listing label omits the wording; its cover says "Relevant for May, 2026 Examination").
- Paper 3 (Taxation) is labelled, and its covers say, valid for May 2026, September 2026 and January 2027.
  Income-tax Law is as amended by the Finance Act 2025 (AY 2026-27).
- Covers read "Relevant for May, 2026", not the literal phrase "Applicable for May 2026 and onwards".
- ICAI also publishes a separate "Applicable for May 2027 Exam Onwards" set. It is not included.
- Taxation corrigenda and May 2026 statutory updates are in the two taxation folders.
  Check ICAI for any newer statutory update before the January 2027 exam.
